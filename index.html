    Clue

    Arrow keys move. Home, Page Up, End and Page Down move diagonally: up left, up right, down left and down right. Enter adds a letter; Enter again on the last letter sends the word. Backspace takes a letter off, Escape clears it.

    How to play

    • Find the lines. Theme words are hidden in the map. Trace one from stop to stop in any direction, diagonals included. Drag across the letters, or tap them one at a time and tap the last letter again. A line can be a phrase with its spaces taken out, like NIGHTOWL.
    • Every letter is used once. Each stop belongs to exactly one line, and lines never cross.
    • The Crosstown. It names the theme, like ODDSANDENDS, and runs from edge to edge.
    • Detours earn fare. Any other word of four letters or more, apart from a theme word with an ending added or taken off, is a detour. Detours can run through lines you’ve found. Four letters earn 1 fare, five earn 2, six earn 3, seven or more earn 4. Every 4 fare becomes a token.
    • A token is a hint. It rings the stops of a line you haven’t found, and a second draws its route. Fare and tokens stay with their puzzle.
    • Seven lines. From Main to Airport, each with its own numbers and progress. Change line, at the top of the list, switches between them.
    • Three stars. One for finishing, one for spending no tokens, one for finding the Crosstown before any other line. A puzzle played again keeps its best.
