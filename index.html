    Clue

    Arrow keys move. Enter adds a letter; Enter again on the last letter sends the word. Backspace takes a letter off, Escape clears it.

    How to play

    • Find the lines. Theme words are hidden in the map. Trace one from stop to stop in any direction, diagonals included. Drag across the letters, or tap them one at a time and tap the last letter again. A line can be a phrase with its spaces taken out, like ONDECK.
    • Every letter is used once. Each stop belongs to exactly one line, and lines never cross.
    • The Crosstown. It names the theme, like JUNKDRAWER, and runs from edge to edge.
    • Detours earn fare. Any other word of four letters or more is a detour. Four letters earn 1 fare, five earn 2, six earn 3, seven or more earn 4. Every 4 fare becomes a token.
    • A token is a hint. It rings the stops of a line you haven’t found, and a second draws its route. Fare and tokens stay with their puzzle.
    • Seven lines. From Main to Airport, each with its own numbers and progress. Change line, at the top of the list, switches between them.
    • Three stars. One for finishing, one for spending no tokens, one for finding the Crosstown before any other line.

    Lines

    Each line is its own set of puzzles. Your progress on every line is kept.
